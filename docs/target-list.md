- 10-50 employees (sweet spot: 20-40)
- B2B or multi-client focus
- Managing Google Ads, Meta, SEO, PPC
- Likely drowning in client reporting
- Owner/Founder is decision-maker
- US-based (timezone + USD)

## Research Template Per Agency

Before emailing, find:
1. **Founder/Owner name** (LinkedIn)
2. **Email** (try firstname@domain or hello@domain)
3. **Recent news** (blog posts, case studies, awards)
4. **Pain signals** (hiring analysts? Posting about reporting?)
