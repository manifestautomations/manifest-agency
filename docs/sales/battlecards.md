# ⚔️ Sales Battlecards: Tier 1 Outreach

**Goal:** Consolidated cheat sheet for closing the first 10 leads.
**Usage:** Review before Discovery Calls.

---

## 1. Disruptive Digital (Maurice Rahmey)
**Focus:** High-End eCommerce (Meta Ads)
**The Pain:** "Attribution Plumbing"
**The Pitch:**
> "Maurice, your strategists are expensive Ex-Facebookers. Why are they cleaning CSVs? We build the 'Attribution Pipeline' that auto-matches Offline Conversions (CAPI) so they can focus on strategy, not data entry."
**The Product:** Automated CAPI / Offline Conversion Import agent.

---

## 2. B2Linked (AJ Wilcox)
**Focus:** B2B LinkedIn Ads (High Volume)
**The Pain:** "Revenue Visibility"
**The Pitch:**
> "AJ, LinkedIn leads are great, but clients churn when they can't see the *deal* in Salesforce. We build the 'Revenue Bridge' that verifies every lead in the CRM and pushes value back to LinkedIn Ads automatically."
**The Product:** LinkedIn -> Salesforce/HubSpot Revenue Sync.

---

## 3. Impactable (Justin Rowe)
**Focus:** High Volume (1,000+ Clients), Small Accounts
**The Pain:** "Churn & Communication at Scale"
**The Pitch:**
> "Justin, scaling to 1,000 clients breaks the human feedback loop. We build a 'Reporting Agent' that writes personalized, plain-English weekly summaries for your bottom 50% of clients, reducing churn without hiring more AMs."
**The Product:** Automated "Human-Like" Weekly Email Reports.

---

## 4. PBJ Marketing (Phillip Reinhardt)
**Focus:** Nonprofits & Advocacy
**The Pain:** "Fragmented Donor Data"
**The Pitch:**
> "Phillip, nonprofits have messy data (Classy, Facebook Fundraisers, Salesforce NPSP). We build the 'Donor Truth Dashboard' that unifies it all so you can prove to the Board exactly where their money went."
**The Product:** Cross-Platform Donor Attribution Dashboard.

---

## 5. Comrade Digital (Ivan Vislavskiy)
**Focus:** Local Services (Lawyers, Plumbers)
**The Pain:** "Lead Quality Disputes"
**The Pitch:**
> "Ivan, local clients fight you on 'Lead Quality'. We automate the matching of CallRail recordings to ServiceTitan booked jobs. You can show them exactly how much revenue you generated, down to the penny."
**The Product:** CallRail <-> Field Service CRM Matcher.

---

## 6. 10X Digital (Holly Rollins)
**Focus:** Student Housing / Real Estate
**The Pain:** "Leasing Attribution"
**The Pitch:**
> "Holly, the gap between Programmatic Ad Spend and 'Signed Leases' in Yardi is where you lose credit. We bridge that gap with an automated 'Occupancy Attribution' agent."
**The Product:** Ad Spend vs. Occupancy Rate Dashboard.

---

## 7. PCG Digital (Glenn Pasch)
**Focus:** Automotive Dealerships
**The Pain:** "VIN-Level ROI"
**The Pitch:**
> "Glenn, dealers care about moving metal. We build the 'VIN Matcher' that connects Google Ad clicks to specific sold VINs in the DMS (CDK/Reynolds). Prove Gross Profit, not just clicks."

---

## 8. Savvy Marketing (Kelly Fitzgerald)
**Focus:** Therapists (Private Practice)
**The Pain:** "Caseload Anxiety"
**The Pitch:**
> "Kelly, therapists don't understand SEO reports. They understand 'Booked Intakes'. We connect your SEO wins directly to their EHR (SimplePractice) so they see '3 New Patients' instead of 'Traffic Up 10%'."

---

## 9. Rock Salt Marketing (Ridge Anderson)
**Focus:** AI SEO / Modern Search
**The Pain:** "Reporting on the Invisible"

**Focus:** Healthcare / HIPAA Compliance
**The Pain:** "Compliance Anxiety"
