# Competitor Deep Dive: Disruptive Digital

**Website:** [disruptivedigital.agency](https://disruptivedigital.agency/)
**CEO:** Maurice Rahmey (Ex-Facebook)
**Focus:** Performance Marketing for eCommerce (Meta Ads Specialist)

---

## 1. Core Offering
"We are strategic partners, not just media buyers."
- **Services:** Paid Social (Meta), Creative Strategy, Measurement/Attribution.
- **Hook:** "Ex-Facebook employees" — they know the platform better than anyone.
- **Client Base:** eCommerce brands needing scale (likely $5M+ revenue).

## 2. Their Strengths
- **Authority:** The "Ex-Facebook" badge is powerful trust signal.
- **Specialization:** They don't do everything. They do paid social extremely well.
- **Data Focus:** They talk heavily about "Measurement" and "Attribution".

## 3. Their Weaknesses (Our Opportunity)
- **Manual "Strategy":** They likely sell "hours of smart people thinking". This doesn't scale linearly.
- **Reporting Burden:** High-end eComm clients demand *obsessive* reporting (daily ROAS, attribution windows). This is likely done manually or via fragile Looker Studio dashboards.
- **Talent Cost:** Hiring ex-Facebook employees is expensive. If they are doing data entry, their margins are bleeding.

## 4. The Manifest Angle (How We Pitch)

**The Hook:**
"Maurice, you hire the best ex-Facebook strategists in the world. Why are they copying and pasting data into spreadsheets?"

**The Problem Statement:**
"Your team's value is *insight* (knowing how the algo works), not *ingestion* (moving data from Ads Manager to a report)."

**The Solution:**
"We build the 'attribution plumbing' that runs automatically. Your strategists get a clean, real-time dashboard every morning. They spend 0 minutes on data prep and 100% of their time on optimization."

**ROI Argument:**
"If your Senior Strategist ($150k/yr) spends 5 hours/week on reporting, you're burning $18,750/year per person on janitor work. We automate that for a flat fee."
