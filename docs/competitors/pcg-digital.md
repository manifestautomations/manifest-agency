# Competitor Deep Dive: PCG Digital

**Website:** [pcgdigital.com](https://pcgdigital.com)
**CEO:** Glenn Pasch
**Focus:** Automotive Dealerships (Tier 2/3 Marketing).
**Size:** 11-50 employees (NJ based).

---

## 1. Core Offering
"We bridge the gap between dealership operations and digital marketing."
- **Services:** Paid Search, SEO, Social, "Internet Sales Mastery" Training.
- **Niche:** Automotive (Car Dealers).
- **Hook:** Founded by the Pasch family (Brian Pasch is an industry legend). They sell *expertise*, not just ads.

## 2. Their Strengths
- **Industry Authority:** The name "Pasch" opens every door in automotive. They host conferences (DMSC).
- **Training + Agency:** They train the dealership's internal team *while* running their ads. This is a brilliant retention strategy.
- **Deep Context:** They understand "VDP views" (Vehicle Detail Page), "Fixed Ops" (Service department), and "Absorption Rates".

## 3. Their Weaknesses (Our Opportunity)
- **DMS Data Silos:** Dealership Management Systems (CDK, Reynolds & Reynolds) are notoriously hostile to open data.
- **Attribution Wars:** Dealers always fight about "Last Click" vs. "Walk-in".
- **Legacy Tech:** Automotive marketing is often stuck on old platforms because "that's how we've always done it".

## 4. The Manifest Angle (How We Pitch)

**The Hook:**
"Glenn, your dealers know VDP views matter. But can they see exactly which $500 ad spend led to the sale of that F-150 in the DMS?"

**The Problem Statement:**
"Dealers live in the DMS (CDK/Reynolds). Your marketing lives in Google Ads. The gap is filled by manual spreadsheets or 'fuzzy math' attribution."

**The Solution:**
"We build the 'VIN-Level Attribution Bridge'. We match the VIN sold in the DMS to the specific ad click that drove the lead. We automate the 'Sold Report' back into Google Ads so the bidding algo optimizes for *Gross Profit*, not just Leads."

**ROI Argument:**
"Dealers run on Gross Profit. If you can show them 'We spent $5k and generated $45k in Gross Profit on these 12 VINs', you never lose that client."

---

## 5. Tactical Approach
- **Outreach Channel:** LinkedIn (Glenn is very active).
- **Specific Pain Point:** "VIN-level attribution without manual matching."
- **Offer:** "A 'VIN Match' prototype for one dealer group."
