# Competitor Deep Dive: B2Linked

**Website:** [b2linked.com](https://b2linked.com)
**CEO:** AJ Wilcox (The "LinkedIn Ads Guy")
**Focus:** Pure-play LinkedIn Ads for B2B.
**Size:** ~12 employees (high revenue per head).

---

## 1. Core Offering
"We only do LinkedIn Ads."
- **Services:** Account Management ($2k+/mo min), Consulting, Training.
- **Hook:** "We manage the world's largest LinkedIn accounts."
- **Tech Stack:** They mention "Proprietary Tools" and a partnership with Supermetrics.

## 2. Their Strengths
- **Brand:** AJ Wilcox IS the brand. He hosts the LinkedIn Ads Show podcast.
- **Niche Dominance:** They are the default choice for big B2B budgets.
- **Efficiency:** They claim to use tools to "create ads in minutes not hours".

## 3. Their Weaknesses (Our Opportunity)
- **Supermetrics Reliance:** They explicitly promote Supermetrics for reporting. This means their clients are likely still dealing with spreadsheets or generic Looker Studio dashboards.
- **Small Team, High Volume:** With only ~12 people managing massive spend, their team is likely stretched on the *analysis* side.
- **Platform Limitation:** They only see LinkedIn. Clients need to see LinkedIn *contextualized* with Hubspot/Salesforce revenue data.

## 4. The Manifest Angle (How We Pitch)

**The Hook:**
"AJ, you built B2Linked on efficiency. But are your account managers still manually checking if 'Lead Gen Form' leads made it into the client's CRM?"

**The Problem Statement:**
"LinkedIn Ads data is isolated. You're great at generating leads, but your clients struggle to prove *revenue* because the data doesn't flow cleanly into their Salesforce/HubSpot."

**The Solution:**
"We build the 'Revenue Bridge'. A silent agent that watches every LinkedIn lead, verifies it in the CRM, and reports back *Offline Conversions* to LinkedIn automatically. Your ROAS goes up because the algo sees the *closed deal*, not just the form fill."

**ROI Argument:**
"You charge premium fees. Offering 'Automated Revenue Attribution' as a value-add makes your agency sticky. We build it, you white-label it."

---

## 5. Tactical Approach
- **Outreach Channel:** LinkedIn DM (he lives there).
- **Specific Pain Point:** "Offline Conversion Import (OCT) automation." LinkedIn's algorithm loves deep funnel data, but uploading CSVs is a pain. We automate it.
- **Offer:** "A 'Revenue Bridge' prototype for one of your clients."
