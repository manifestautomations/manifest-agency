# Competitor Deep Dive: Savvy Marketing Services

**Website:** [Savvy Marketing (on Productive Therapist directory)](https://directory.productivetherapist.com/portland/services/savvy-marketing-services)
**CEO:** Kelly Fitzgerald
**Focus:** Mental Health Professionals (Therapists, Counselors).
**Size:** Small/Boutique (Likely <10 employees).

---

## 1. Core Offering
"Data-driven marketing for therapists."
- **Services:** SEO, Website Optimization, Content.
- **Niche:** Private Practice Therapists.
- **Hook:** "I'm married to a therapist. I get it." (Authenticity play).

## 2. Their Strengths
- **Hyper-Niche:** She speaks "Therapist". She knows about Psychology Today profiles, HIPAA compliance, and "private pay" vs. insurance.
- **Trust:** Therapists are skeptical of "marketing gurus". Kelly's personal connection (spouse) builds instant trust.
- **Education First:** She does podcasts ("Private Practice Workshop") and teaches.

## 3. Their Weaknesses (Our Opportunity)
- **Scale:** Small team means reporting is likely manual and time-consuming.
- **Client Technical Literacy:** Therapists are generally *very* non-technical. They need simple, reassuring reports, not complex dashboards.
- **SEO Reporting is Dry:** Showing a therapist a ranking report for "anxiety counselor Portland" is okay, but showing *booked intakes* is better.

## 4. The Manifest Angle (How We Pitch)

**The Hook:**
"Kelly, you know therapists care about *intakes*, not 'SERP rankings'. But linking your SEO wins to their EHR (Electronic Health Record) is a pain, right?"

**The Problem Statement:**
"Therapists use specialized EHRs (SimplePractice, TherapyNotes). Your SEO data lives in Ahrefs/Google. The disconnect makes it hard to prove that your SEO work actually filled their caseload."

**The Solution:**
"We build the 'Caseload Filler Dashboard'. It pulls organic traffic data and correlates it with 'New Client Calls' (via call tracking) and 'Intake Forms'. It shows the therapist: 'Your SEO investment filled 3 slots this week.'"

**ROI Argument:**
"Therapists measure life in 'Client Hours'. If you can prove your marketing added 5 billable hours/week, they will never cancel. We automate that proof."

---

## 5. Tactical Approach
- **Outreach Channel:** Email (she is approachable) or Instagram DM (active there).
- **Specific Pain Point:** "Proving SEO ROI to non-technical therapists."
- **Offer:** "A 'Simple ROI' dashboard for one of your private practice clients."
