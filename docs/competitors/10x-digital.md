# Competitor Deep Dive: 10X Digital

**Website:** [10xdigitalinc.com](https://10xdigitalinc.com)
**CEO:** Holly Rollins
**Focus:** Student Housing, Real Estate, Programmatic Advertising.
**Size:** 11-50 employees (Greenville, SC).

---

## 1. Core Offering
"Precision-targeted programmatic advertising for Student Housing."
- **Services:** Programmatic Ads (Geo-fencing), PR, Content Marketing.
- **Niche:** Student Housing (REITs), Real Estate.
- **Hook:** "20+ years experience, 100,000+ beds marketed."

## 2. Their Strengths
- **Niche Dominance:** Student Housing is a specialized vertical. They know the "Leasing Cycle" (Pre-lease vs. Fill).
- **Tech Forward:** They openly talk about "AI-driven programmatic" and "Geo-targeting".
- **PR Integration:** They combine paid ads with PR (Holly's background), which is rare for performance shops.

## 3. Their Weaknesses (Our Opportunity)
- **Reporting Fragmentation:** Student Housing reporting is notoriously split between "Ad Clicks" (Marketing) and "Signed Leases" (Property Management Software like Yardi/Entrata).
- **Leasing Seasonality:** The "Turn" (August move-in) creates massive spikes in workload. Manual reporting during "The Turn" is a nightmare.
- **Attribution:** Programmatic (View-through conversions) is hard to prove to property owners who just want "Leases Signed".

## 4. The Manifest Angle (How We Pitch)

**The Hook:**
"Holly, you're running programmatic for Student Housing. But can your clients see how many 'Walk-ins' were influenced by your Geofencing ads in real-time?"

**The Problem Statement:**
"Student Housing REITs live in Yardi/Entrata. Your ads live in a DSP (Demand Side Platform). The gap between 'Ad Spend' and 'Occupancy Rate' is where you lose credit for your work."

**The Solution:**
"We build the 'Occupancy Attribution Engine'. It pulls real-time leasing data from Yardi and overlays it with your Programmatic spend. You can show a property manager: 'We spent $500 yesterday, and 3 leases were signed today by people who saw our ads.'"

**ROI Argument:**
"REITs care about *Occupancy %* and *Cost Per Bed*. If you automate that report, you become their Revenue Partner, not just a vendor expense."

## 5. Tactical Approach
- **Outreach Channel:** Email (she is active in local business councils) + LinkedIn.
- **Specific Pain Point:** "Yardi/Entrata integration with Programmatic reporting."
- **Offer:** "A 'Lease Attribution' dashboard prototype."
