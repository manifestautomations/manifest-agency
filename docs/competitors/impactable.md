# Competitor Deep Dive: Impactable

**Website:** [impactable.com](https://impactable.com)
**CEO:** Justin Rowe
**Focus:** High-volume LinkedIn Ads + Demand Gen.
**Size:** 84+ employees (Scaled rapidly).
**Tech:** "DemandSense" (Proprietary software).

---

## 1. Core Offering
"The largest LinkedIn Ads agency in the world."
- **Services:** Paid Ads (LinkedIn first, then Google/Programmatic), Demand Gen.
- **Hook:** "Launch, fix, and scale confidently."
- **Scale:** They serve 1,000+ clients. This is a volume shop, not a boutique.

## 2. Their Strengths
- **Volume:** They have seen more data than anyone.
- **Software-Led:** They built "DemandSense" to handle scheduling, budget caps, and frequency. They are *already* thinking like an automation company.
- **Content:** Justin Rowe is a content machine. They dominate the "LinkedIn Ads" SEO and thought leadership.

## 3. Their Weaknesses (Our Opportunity)
- **Quality Control at Scale:** Clutch reviews mention "mis-naming customers" and "lack of reporting". This is the classic breaking point of scaling a service business to 1,000 clients.
- **The "Black Box" Problem:** Their proprietary software (DemandSense) might be great, but clients often feel disconnected if they can't see the raw data in their *own* BI tools.
- **Churn:** High volume agencies usually burn through small clients who don't see immediate ROI. Better onboarding/reporting reduces churn.

## 4. The Manifest Angle (How We Pitch)

**The Hook:**
"Justin, you built DemandSense to solve *your* internal problems. But your churned clients are leaving because they don't understand the 'Black Box'."

**The Problem Statement:**
"Scaling to 1,000 clients breaks the human feedback loop. Your account managers are overwhelmed, and clients feel ignored. Automated reporting isn't enough—you need Automated *Communication*."

**The Solution:**
"We build the 'Client Portal Agent'. It doesn't just show a graph; it writes a weekly plain-English summary of *why* the graph moved, using your best strategist's logic. It's high-touch service at low-touch cost."

**ROI Argument:**
"If you reduce churn by 1% across 1,000 clients paying $2k/mo, that's $20k/month in saved revenue. Our system costs less than one junior AM."

---

## 5. Tactical Approach
- **Outreach Channel:** LinkedIn (obviously).
- **Specific Pain Point:** "Churn reduction via automated high-touch reporting."
- **Offer:** "A 'Summary Agent' that writes weekly updates for your bottom 50% of clients."
