# Competitor Deep Dive: Rock Salt Marketing

**Website:** [rocksaltmarketing.com](https://rocksaltmarketing.com)
**CEO:** Ridge Anderson (Co-Founder)
**Focus:** SEO, Paid Media, Web Design (Recently launched "AI SEO Services").
**Size:** Small/Mid-Market (Utah based).

---

## 1. Core Offering
"We help you rank in the AI Era."
- **Services:** SEO, Paid Media, Design.
- **Hook:** "AI SEO Services for LLM Search Visibility" (They are pivoting to Answer Engine Optimization).
- **Vibe:** "Cooperative" model implies a team-first, potentially employee-owned culture.

## 2. Their Strengths
- **Forward-Thinking:** They are already selling "LLM Visibility" (Perplexity/ChatGPT optimization). Most agencies are still stuck on "Google Rankings".
- **Local Roots:** Strong presence in Utah/Nashville.
- **Agility:** Being smaller allows them to pivot to AI faster than the big guys.

## 3. Their Weaknesses (Our Opportunity)
- **"AI SEO" is Hard to Prove:** How do you report on "Brand Mentions in ChatGPT"? Standard SEO tools (Ahrefs/Semrush) don't track this well yet.
- **Manual Reporting:** Tracking LLM visibility currently involves manual testing ("Hey ChatGPT, recommend a plumber in SLC"). This is unscalable.
- **Education Burden:** They have to teach every client what "LLM Optimization" means. That eats up account management time.

## 4. The Manifest Angle (How We Pitch)

**The Hook:**
"Ridge, I saw you launched AI SEO services. Love the move. But how are you *reporting* on ChatGPT visibility to clients without manual screenshots?"

**The Problem Statement:**
"Standard SEO tools don't track LLMs. Your team is likely manually prompting ChatGPT/Perplexity to check client visibility. That's a massive time suck and hard to quantify."

**The Solution:**
"We build the 'LLM Visibility Tracker'. It's an automated agent that prompts ChatGPT/Claude/Perplexity daily with buyer-intent keywords, checks if your client is mentioned, and logs the 'Share of Voice'. You get a graph, not a screenshot."

**ROI Argument:**
"You are selling a cutting-edge service. You need cutting-edge reporting to back it up. We build the 'Perplexity Rank Tracker' that sets you apart."
