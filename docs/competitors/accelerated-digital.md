# Competitor Deep Dive: Accelerated Digital Media

**Website:** [accelerateddigitalmedia.com](https://accelerateddigitalmedia.com)
**CEO:** Tellef Lundevall (Ex-Google)
**Focus:** Healthcare, Digital Health, HIPAA Compliance.
**Size:** 20-50 employees (Chicago).

---

## 1. Core Offering
"We grow digital health brands."
- **Services:** Paid Search, Paid Social, Programmatic, SEO.
- **Niche:** Healthcare, Telehealth, DTC Health.
- **Hook:** "HIPAA-Compliant Growth." They solve the privacy vs. performance paradox.

## 2. Their Strengths
- **Privacy First:** They are experts in the "Freshpaint" stack (HIPAA-compliant data layers). This is a massive moat.
- **Ex-Google DNA:** Tellef's background gives them "Insider" credibility.
- **Sophisticated Analytics:** They talk heavily about "Attribution Modeling" and "GA4 for Healthcare". They are not afraid of data.

## 3. Their Weaknesses (Our Opportunity)
- **Compliance Friction:** HIPAA compliance usually means "less data". Ad platforms hate this. Their team likely spends hours manually auditing tracking setups to ensure tracking pixels aren't scraping PHI (Protected Health Information).
- **Attribution Gaps:** Telehealth funnels are long (Ad -> Quiz -> Consult -> Rx). Connecting the "Rx Fill" back to the "Ad Click" without breaking HIPAA is hard.
- **Audit Fatigue:** They offer "Free Audits" as a lead magnet. This is high effort/low reward if done manually.

## 4. The Manifest Angle (How We Pitch)

**The Hook:**
"Tellef, you're the leader in HIPAA-compliant growth. But how much time does your team spend manually auditing GTM containers to ensure no PHI leaks?"

**The Problem Statement:**
"Healthcare marketing lives in fear of a HIPAA violation. Your team has to be 'Compliance Cops' first and 'Marketers' second. That manual oversight slows down optimization."

**The Solution:**
"We build the 'Compliance Sentinel'. It's an automated agent that scans every landing page and GTM tag daily. It attempts to trigger tracking pixels with dummy PHI. If a pixel fires illegaly, it alerts you immediately. You get 24/7 peace of mind without the manual audit."

**ROI Argument:**
"One HIPAA violation costs millions. Our 'Sentinel Agent' costs less than your liability insurance deductible."
