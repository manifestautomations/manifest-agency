# The Problem

Oak Street Dental was missing 35% of patient calls. Their front desk was overwhelmed with scheduling, insurance verification, and in-person check-ins. Every missed call was a potential patient (value: $450) lost to a competitor.

# The Solution

We deployed a Voice AI Receptionist using Vapi.ai and Twilio.
- **Immediate Response:** The AI answers instantly, 24/7.
- **Integration:** It connects directly to their Practice Management Software (Dentrix) to book appointments in real-time.
- **Escalation:** Complex medical questions are intelligently routed to a human nurse.

# The Outcome

In the first 30 days:
- **0% Missed Calls:** Every patient spoke to an "agent".
- **32 New Appointments:** booked automatically after hours.
- **$14,400 Revenue:** directly attributed to the AI agent.
