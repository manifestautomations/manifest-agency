# The Problem

Cooking apps are broken.
You have to touch your phone with wet, greasy hands to scroll down the recipe.
You have to unlock your phone every time the screen dims.
It breaks the flow of cooking.

# The Solution

We built **Sous Chef**, a hands-free, voice-first kitchen assistant.
- **Vision:** Snap a photo of your fridge. The AI identifies ingredients and suggests recipes.
- **Voice:** "Hey Chef, what's the next step?" or "How much cumin?"
- **State Management:** The AI remembers exactly where you are in the recipe.

# The Tech Stack

- **Vision:** GPT-4o Vision for ingredient analysis.
- **Voice:** Vapi.ai for low-latency conversation.
- **Backend:** Supabase for recipe storage and user preferences.
- **Frontend:** React Native (Expo) for cross-platform mobile deployment.

# The Outcome

We went from "Idea" to "TestFlight" in **two weeks**.
This project served as a proving ground for our **Voice AI capabilities**, which we now deploy for enterprise clients (e.g., Dental Receptionists).
It proved that "Voice" is not just for customer service—it's the future of hands-busy interfaces.
