**Headline:** Stop building your agency on Zapier.

Most agencies I talk to are stuck in "Spaghetti Automation".
You have 50 Zaps. Nobody knows what they do. If one breaks, your entire client onboarding stops.

We call this **Stage 1: The Band-Aid Phase.**
It works when you have 5 clients. It kills you when you have 50.

If you want to scale, you need to graduate to **Stage 3: The Platform Phase.**
- No more "Zaps".
- Dedicated data ingestion (Airbyte).
- Central data warehouse (BigQuery/Snowflake).
- Logic defined in code (dbt), not drag-and-drop bubbles.

This is how software companies operate. Why are agencies still running their ops like it's 2015?

It maps out exactly how to move from "Manual Chaos" to a "Self-Driving Agency".
