# The 6-Second Filter

Did you know the average recruiter spends just **6 seconds** scanning a resume?
They look for:
1.  Big Tech logos (Google, Meta).
2.  Ivy League schools.
3.  Job titles that match exactly.

If you don't fit that pattern, you go in the trash. Even if you are the best coder in the world.

# The "Keyword" Trap

Legacy ATS (Applicant Tracking Systems) made this worse.
They look for keywords.
- JD says "React.js"?
- Resume says "Frontend Architecture"?
- **Result:** Rejected.

This is "Symbolic AI" from the 1990s. It's dumb.

# Enter Reasoning Agents (DeepSeek R1)

The new wave of AI Recruiters (like the ones we build at Manifest) don't just "match keywords". They **reason**.

**The AI Thought Process:**
> "This candidate doesn't list 'React', but they built a complex dashboard using Next.js 14. Therefore, they are an expert in React. They also founded a startup, which means they have 'Grind'—a trait the client explicitly asked for."

## The Vapi Interview

It doesn't stop at the resume.
Our agents call the candidate immediately.
- **Latency:** <500ms.
- **Voice:** Indistinguishable from human.
- **Goal:** Verify communication skills and technical depth.

"Hey John, I see you used Supabase in your last project. Why did you pick that over Firebase?"

The AI listens to the answer. It detects hesitation. It detects confidence. It detects BS.

# The Future: Agent-to-Agent Hiring

Soon, you won't apply for jobs.
Your **Personal Agent** will talk to the **Company's Recruiter Agent**.
They will negotiate salary, role, and culture fit in milliseconds.
You will just wake up with an interview booked on your calendar.

The resume is dead. Long live the Agent.
