## How It Works
1. **You speak** naturally to the AI.
2. **Vapi.ai** transcribes your voice in real-time.
3. **Our LLM** (running on Next.js Edge) decides if you want to book or ask a question.
4. **Tools** are called to check our real-time calendar availability.
5. **ElevenLabs** generates a human-like voice response.

## Why This Matters
- **Zero Missed Calls:** It scales infinitely.
- **Instant Booking:** No "press 1 for appointments".
- **Context Aware:** It knows your business hours and services.
