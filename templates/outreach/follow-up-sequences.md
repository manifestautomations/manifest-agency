# Manifest Automations: Follow-Up Email Sequences

**Philosophy:**
*   **Be Persistent, Not Annoying.** 4-5 touchpoints is standard.
*   **Add Value.** Don't just say "checking in". Send a resource, an idea, or a relevant case study.
*   **Short is Sweet.** Mobile-friendly, 2-3 sentences max for bumps.

---

## Sequence A: Cold Outreach Follow-Ups
*Use these after sending the initial "Cold Email" if you get no reply.*

### Email 2: The "Quick Bump" (Day 3)
**Subject:** Re: [Original Subject]

Hi [Name],

I know inboxes get buried. Just wanted to float this to the top.

Did you have a chance to read my note about automating your client data onboarding? I think we could save your team about 20 hours a week.

Best,
[Your Name]

### Email 3: The "Value Add" (Day 7)
**Subject:** Idea for [Company Name] reporting

Hi [Name],

I was thinking about how [Company Name] handles [Specific Report Type/Data].

Most agencies I talk to are manually copying data from CSVs into Excel for this. If you're doing that, we can build a script to automate it in about 3 days.

Here’s a 1-min video of how it works: [Link to generic demo/Loom]

Worth a quick chat?

Best,
[Your Name]

### Email 4: The "Break Up" (Day 14)
**Subject:** Permission to close your file?

Hi [Name],

I haven't heard back, so I assume automating your data pipeline isn't a priority right now.

I'm going to close your file on my end so I stop pestering you. If you ever want to stop the manual data entry grind, you know where to find me.

Best,
[Your Name]

## Sequence B: Post-Discovery Call
*Use these after you've had a call/demo, but they haven't booked the next step or gone silent.*

### Email 1: The "Recap & Next Step" (Same Day as Call)
**Subject:** Manifest x [Company Name] - Next Steps

Hi [Name],

Great chatting today. It sounds like the manual data cleaning for [Client X] is costing you about [X] hours/week. Ouch.

**Proposed Next Step:**
I recommend a **Technical Discovery ($5k)**. We'll map out exactly how to automate that workflow so you never have to open a CSV again.

Attached is the SOW. Can we get this signed by Friday to hold your slot for next week?

Best,
[Your Name]

### Email 2: The "Gentle Nudge" (Day 3 post-call)
**Subject:** Any questions on the proposal?

Hi [Name],

Just checking to see if you had any questions on the Discovery SOW I sent over?

I have some engineering capacity opening up next week and would love to allocate it to [Company Name] if you're ready to move.

Best,
[Your Name]

### Email 3: The "Case Study" (Day 7 post-call)
**Subject:** How [Similar Client] saved 40hrs/week

Hi [Name],

You mentioned you were worried about [Objection, e.g., security/downtime].

I wanted to share how we handled that for [Other Client]. We built a parallel pipeline so their existing reporting never went down during the transition.

[Link to Case Study/One-Pager]

Let me know if you're ready to get started.

Best,
[Your Name]

## Sequence C: Contract/SOW Sent

Just making sure the DocuSign didn't land in spam.

Let me know if you need me to adjust anything in the scope. Otherwise, once that's signed, we can schedule the kickoff for [Date].

Best,
[Your Name]

Are we still good to move forward with the [Project Name]? If you can get the SOW signed today, we can keep that [Date] kickoff slot. Otherwise, we might need to push it back a week.

Best,
[Your Name]
