## Usage Notes

1. **Personalize the first line** — Don't just mail-merge. Mention something specific (a recent case study they posted, an award, their client list).

2. **Subject line testing** — Send 5 with Subject A, 5 with Subject B. Track open rates.

3. **Timing:**
   - Send first touch: Tuesday-Thursday, 9-11 AM local time
   - Follow-up #1: 3-4 days later
   - Follow-up #2: 7 days after that
   - Stop after 3 touches (don't be annoying)

4. **Calendly link** — Always include. Remove friction.

5. **P.S. line** — High-converting element. Use it.
