# 🎨 Manifest Agency - Design System (v1.0)

**Philosophy:** Brutalist, High-Contrast, "Engineering-First".
**Voice:** Direct, confident, no-nonsense.

---

## 🎨 Color Palette

### Primary (The "Terminal" Look)
- **Void Black:** `#0A0A0A` (Backgrounds)
- **Code White:** `#F0F0F0` (Text)
- **Alert Orange:** `#FF4500` (CTAs, Errors, Highlights)

### Secondary (The "Data" Look)
- **Matrix Green:** `#00FF41` (Success, Metrics)
- **Concrete Gray:** `#888888` (Borders, De-emphasized text)
- **Blueprint Blue:** `#0047AB` (Links, Info)

---

## 🔠 Typography

### Headings (Display)
**Font:** `Inter` or `Space Grotesk` (Google Fonts)
- **H1:** 64px / 700 Bold / Tight Tracking (-0.02em)
- **H2:** 48px / 600 SemiBold
- **H3:** 32px / 500 Medium

### Body (Readability)
**Font:** `JetBrains Mono` or `Roboto Mono` (Monospace for that "dev" feel)
- **Body:** 16px / 400 Regular / 1.5 Line Height
- **Small:** 14px / 400 Regular

---

## 📐 Spacing & Layout

**Grid:** 8pt Grid System.
- **xs:** 4px
- **sm:** 8px
- **md:** 16px
- **lg:** 24px
- **xl:** 32px
- **2xl:** 64px

**Container Max-Width:** 1200px (Centered).

---

## 🧩 Components

### 1. The "Terminal" Card
*Used for: Services, Case Studies.*
- **Bg:** Void Black
- **Border:** 1px Solid Concrete Gray
- **Hover:** Border turns Alert Orange, slight lift.
- **Content:** Monospace header, icon, brief text.

### 2. The "Execute" Button
*Used for: Primary CTAs.*
- **Bg:** Alert Orange
- **Text:** Void Black (Bold)
- **Shape:** Sharp corners (0px radius).
- **Hover:** Invert (Black Bg, Orange Text + Border).

### 3. The "Data" Table
*Used for: Pricing, Comparisons.*
- **Header:** Uppercase, tracked out, Gray border bottom.
- **Rows:** Zebra striping (very subtle opacity).
- **Font:** Monospace numbers.

---

## 📸 Imagery Style
- **Subject:** abstract data flows, server racks, clean code screenshots.
- **Treatment:** Black & White or Duotone (Black + Orange).
- **Avoid:** Generic stock photos of people shaking hands.
