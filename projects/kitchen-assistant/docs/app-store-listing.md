## 2. Description

**Promotional Text (170 chars - iOS only):**
Stop throwing away food. Track your pantry, get expiration alerts, and find recipes for what you already have. The smart kitchen OS for home chefs.

**Full Description:**

**Your Kitchen, Organized.**
Kitchen Assistant is the all-in-one culinary operating system designed to help you save money, reduce food waste, and answer the daily question: "What's for dinner?"

**Why You'll Love Kitchen Assistant:**

🥑 **Smart Pantry Tracking**
Never guess if you have milk again. Scan barcodes to instantly add items to your digital pantry. We automatically pull nutrition info, images, and shelf life data.

⏰ **Stop Food Waste**
Get "Usage Insights" and expiration alerts before your food goes bad. Our "Waste Watch" feature highlights the items you frequently toss, helping you buy smarter.

🛒 **Intelligent Shopping Lists**
Items are automatically sorted by aisle (Produce, Dairy, Spices) so you can get in and out of the store faster. Running low? One tap moves an item from your Pantry to your Shopping List.

🍳 **Cook With What You Have**
Find recipes based on the ingredients sitting in your fridge right now. See exactly what you're missing for any dish with our "Pantry Match" score.

**Key Features:**
*   **Barcode Scanner:** Lightning-fast entry with OpenFoodFacts integration.
*   **Expiration Tracking:** Traffic light system (Green/Yellow/Red) for freshness.
*   **Household Sync:** Share your pantry and shopping list with family or roommates in real-time.
*   **Dietary Profiles:** Set allergens and dietary preferences (Vegan, Gluten-Free) to get safe recipe suggestions.
*   **Offline Mode:** Works even in the back of the grocery store without signal.

**Download Kitchen Assistant today and start cooking smarter.**
